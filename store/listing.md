# WarmSlider

Give your eyes a more comfortable screen after dark.

WarmSlider provides straightforward controls for display warmth and brightness,
remembers your preferences, and returns your display to neutral in one click.

- Warmth from 2500 K to 6500 K
- Brightness from 20% to 100%
- Multiple-display support on X11
- Native GNOME night-light integration on Wayland
- No accounts, analytics, advertisements, or data collection

Brightness support depends on what the desktop session and display expose.
